# Image prompts: The Vibe Coding Cookbook deck

The deck is complete without these pictures. Every diagram, chart and icon on the slides is drawn inside the HTML
file, so it works offline and can be emailed as one file. These prompts are **optional upgrades** for the eight
chapter dividers, the title slide and the closing slide.

**How to use them**
1. Generate each picture with any image model. Use the house style below plus the prompt.
2. Save it under the file name given (PNG or JPG), at the size given.
3. Hand the files back. They can then be embedded into the single HTML file, so it still works offline.

## House style (put this in front of every prompt)
> Dark charcoal background #1A1A24. One EY-yellow #FFE600 accent; teal #27ACAA and orange #FF6D00 only as secondary
> colours. Minimal flat or lightly isometric line illustration, precise geometry, generous negative space,
> presentation-ready, not photorealistic. No text, letters, numbers, logos, brand marks, real people or faces.

No text in the pictures: image models garble lettering, and the slide already carries the words.

## Chapter dividers: 8 square illustrations (560 × 560 px, 1:1)
Each one replaces the line icon in the framed panel on the right of a divider slide.

### 1. `d0-pen-and-paper.png` · Part 0 · Pen and paper
- **Purpose:** make preparation before prompting feel concrete and deliberate.
- **Prompt:** One crisp sheet of paper with a folded corner and a single pen crossing it diagonally, three short
  planning marks on the page, precise geometric construction, white linework with one EY-yellow accent on the pen,
  subtle depth.
- **Avoid:** hands, faces, gradients that reduce contrast, clutter.

### 2. `d1-who-owns-what.png` · Part 1 · Who owns what
- **Purpose:** show a clear boundary between the work you own and the work you delegate to the agent.
- **Prompt:** Two balanced responsibility zones separated by a firm vertical boundary and a central lock; an
  abstract key shape on the human side in muted orange, connected nodes on the agent side in teal; the lock
  highlighted once in EY-yellow; geometric and spare.
- **Avoid:** humanoid robots, decorative circuitry, clutter.

### 3. `d2-lay-the-foundation.png` · Part 2 · Lay the foundation
- **Purpose:** show that later work rests on an ordered foundation.
- **Prompt:** Three precise foundation courses stacked from a broad base to a narrow top, a single downward guide
  arrow showing top-down construction, white linework with the base course in EY-yellow, architectural clarity,
  strong silhouette.
- **Avoid:** buildings, construction workers, rubble, busy backgrounds.

### 4. `d3-the-components.png` · Part 3 · The components
- **Purpose:** show the model as one element inside a configured system.
- **Prompt:** One central square module connected cleanly to eight distinct surrounding modules, balanced radial
  geometry, the central module accented in EY-yellow and one secondary inner module in teal, white connecting lines,
  like a premium technical diagram without labels.
- **Avoid:** robot heads, brains, glowing sci-fi effects, dense circuitry.

### 5. `d4-running-the-workflow.png` · Part 4 · Running the workflow
- **Purpose:** show one task moving through a controlled verification loop.
- **Prompt:** A clean closed loop made from two directional arrows, two small human approval gates in orange, a
  central verified check mark in EY-yellow, teal on the returning path, strong circular motion.
- **Avoid:** people, humanoid robots, conveyor belts, excessive arrows.

### 6. `d5-guardrails.png` · Part 5 · Guardrails
- **Purpose:** show deterministic protection around the workflow.
- **Prompt:** A strong shield held exactly between two geometric guard rails, a simple check mark inside the shield,
  white linework with one EY-yellow shield accent, a small blocked threat outside the rails in red #FF4136,
  symmetrical and restrained.
- **Avoid:** medieval imagery, weapons, cyberpunk effects, clutter.

### 7. `d6-principles.png` · Part 6 · Principles
- **Purpose:** show principles as a direction, not a rigid template.
- **Prompt:** A precise compass with one decisive needle pointing forward and several quiet alternative bearings,
  white linework, the chosen needle in EY-yellow, calm and confident.
- **Avoid:** maps, landscapes, ornate nautical styling.

### 8. `d7-your-turn.png` · Part 7 · Your turn
- **Purpose:** show the workflow being handed to the audience to adapt.
- **Prompt:** Two abstract geometric hands passing a small modular workflow kit from left to right, the giving side
  in muted orange, the receiving side in teal, the kit accented once in EY-yellow, clean angular forms.
- **Avoid:** handshake imagery, humanoid robots, clutter.

## Backgrounds: 2 full-bleed pictures (1920 × 1080 px, 16:9)
They replace the prism background behind the title and the closing slide.

### 9. `title-background.jpg` · Title slide
- **Purpose:** open the talk on practical, controlled engineering.
- **Prompt:** An abstract prism field in the EY style, one luminous folded plane or ribbon moving from the lower centre
  toward the right edge, EY-yellow as the single bright accent with restrained warm orange reflections, subtle
  translucent geometry suggesting layers and controlled motion. Keep the **left 60% dark and quiet** for the title.
- **Avoid:** code screenshots, robots, rainbow colours, busy texture, bright details on the left.

### 10. `closing-background.jpg` · Closing slide ("Start this week")
- **Purpose:** end with forward motion and room for three short actions.
- **Prompt:** An abstract prism field in the EY style, three folded planes converging into one confident forward path
  toward the upper right, EY-yellow as the dominant accent with very restrained teal and orange edge reflections,
  a strong sense of hand-off and momentum. Keep the **left 65% dark and readable**.
- **Avoid:** arrows with labels, confetti, celebratory clichés, clutter.
